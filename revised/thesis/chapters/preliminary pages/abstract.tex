\section*{Abstract}

This thesis introduces the application of Deep Equilibrium Nets (DEQNs) to the Analytic Climate Economy (ACE), aiming to lift the curse of dimensionality present in traditional solution methods. To the best of my knowledge, it is one of the first applications of this solution method to integrated assessment models (IAMs). It is also one of the first to contextualize this method in terms of the reinforcement learning literature. With that, this research is a step toward making it possible to relax restrictive assumptions typically necessary in existing models. However, the findings demonstrate that DEQNs, as mapped in this thesis, struggle to approximate the equilibrium conditions of ACE, suggesting that the solution method, as applied here, is not very robust. The thesis identifies areas for improvement, particularly in the normalization of inputs, and suggests exploring more effective algorithms inspired by advances in reinforcement learning.
