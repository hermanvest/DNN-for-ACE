\subsubsection{The Climate Model}
For the purposes of this thesis, I chose to lie close to the climate emulator in DICE. DICE has a carbon cycle with three reservoirs denoted as \( \mathbf{M}_t \in \mathbb{R}^3 \). This models atmospheric content \( M_{1,t} \), upper ocean and biosphere \( M_{2,t} \), and finally the deep ocean \( M_{3,t} \). As a result, the transition matrix is a 3x3 matrix \(\bm{\Phi} \in \mathbb{R}^{3\times 3}\). Finally, I will be using a two-layer temperature model, which means that the temperature vector is two dimensional: \(\bm{T}_t \in \mathbb{R}^2\).
